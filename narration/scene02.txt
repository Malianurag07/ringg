Here is the cell, in the order the disc travels. First, a sensor counts every disc entering. Then Camera 1, which decides which side is up. Next, the flipping machine, which turns over any disc that is burr side up. Then Camera 2, which measures how far the disc is rotated. After that comes the robot, which picks the disc up, turns it to the correct angle and places it into its slot. Discs the system is unsure about go to a reject bin instead. A line computer with a GPU sits behind all of this and makes every decision.

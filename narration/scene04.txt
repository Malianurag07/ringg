The decision is simple. If the rollover side is up, the disc passes straight through the flipping machine. If the burr side is up, the flipping machine turns the disc over. If the system is not confident, it never guesses. It sends the disc to the reject bin. The system tracks each disc by the belt position count, so the right flip decision is always applied to the right disc. After this machine, every disc that continues is rollover side up.

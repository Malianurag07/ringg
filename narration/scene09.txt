Why does this need a GPU? Two 12-megapixel cameras produce a lot of data for every disc. The line must run fast, around the clock, with every stage working at the same time: while one disc is being judged, the next is being captured and the previous one's instruction is being sent. The ML model that decides the side is heavy arithmetic. The GPU takes it off the processor, so processing time stays steady even under full load. It also leaves room for larger models or more cameras later. Everything runs on site and offline, because the disc designs are confidential.

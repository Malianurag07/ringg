Camera 1 is a 12-megapixel industrial camera, which gives a very detailed view of each disc. The PLC triggers it as the disc passes, and records the belt position at that instant. Lighting matters a lot here. A low-angle light, shining across the disc from the side, makes the raised burr edge stand out clearly, while the rounded rollover edge stays soft. The camera sits in an enclosure so that factory light never changes the picture. The line computer first finds the disc and crops it, then ML and OpenCV methods, chosen after testing on real discs, decide which side is up. Alongside the answer, the system gives a confidence score.
